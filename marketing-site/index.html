02 / Method

Start with the business problem,
not the model.

Each stage creates a decision point. We move forward only when ownership, authorized inputs, review, and success criteria are clear.

  1. 01

    Align on the operating problem

    Define the current process, friction, owner, users, and why the problem matters.

  2. 02

    Select a pilot workflow

    Choose one frequent, observable task with authorized inputs and a named reviewer.

  3. 03

    Train and build together

    Combine role-based learning with co-creation, human review, documentation, and fallback.

  4. 04

    Validate and decide

    Use actual workflow evidence to continue, adjust, or stop—without forcing a success story.

04 / Diagnostic boundary

What a first conversation
does—and does not—do.

20 minutes, not a free solution design

The first conversation clarifies the workflow, owner, authorized samples, review point, constraints, and a reasonable next step.

No guaranteed implementation or ROI

A diagnosis does not promise adoption, savings, revenue, or a finished system. Those claims require baseline data and observed use.

No sensitive data in the first contact

Do not send passwords, tokens, cookies, confidential customer records, or material you are not authorized to share.
