# Content and reuse boundary

## What MIT covers

The root `LICENSE` applies to repository software: source code, configuration, tests, and generic operational documentation authored for this project.

## What MIT does not automatically cover

Unless a file or page states otherwise, the MIT License does not grant rights to:

- third-party posts, videos, screenshots, images, logos, documentation, product descriptions, or quoted material;
- the underlying claims and creative expression of linked tutorial/case sources;
- names, marks, or brand identity belonging to Kunlun Growth AI or a third party;
- personal data, customer material, submitted works, or proprietary examples;
- editorial tutorial/case text where the contributor has not expressly licensed it for redistribution.

Source links and author labels are attribution and verification aids, not evidence that the source work is open-licensed.

## Safe reuse rules

1. Reuse the MIT-covered software while retaining the MIT notice.
2. Replace or separately license editorial content, brand assets, and third-party material before commercial redistribution.
3. Keep original source links, author/organization labels, and claim boundaries when you publish an authorized adaptation.
4. Do not present vendor/customer claims as independently verified results.
5. Remove material promptly when a rightsholder establishes a valid ownership or authorization concern.

## Submissions

The public submission form requires the submitter to confirm truthfulness and public rights, but Formspree delivery does not itself prove ownership. Human review is required before publication. Do not publish customer secrets, personal data, unlicensed media, or confidential business information.

## Rights and takedown contact

The operating deployment must configure a monitored Formspree endpoint or `NEXT_PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL` before commercial launch. A rightsholder should identify the URL, the protected work, their relationship to it, and the requested correction or removal. The operator should preserve the request privately, remove clearly unauthorized material while reviewing, and document the resolution.
